Why Arha Group?

Where Heritage Meets Gold

Arha Group stands at the intersection of luxury, tradition, and innovation. Our exclusive gold-woven collections redefine luxury by transforming 22-carat gold into extraordinary works of wearable art.

We champion heritage craftsmanship, empower artisans, and create heirloom-quality pieces that celebrate cultural excellence. Every Arha creation represents sophistication, status, and a legacy designed to endure beyond time.

For those who value rarity, heritage, and distinction, Arha Group offers an experience that is truly golden.

About the Founder

Swathi N, a seasoned corporate trainer and visionary entrepreneur, founded Arha Group with a dream that was both deeply personal and profoundly meaningful—to bring back India's golden era through innovation, heritage, and craftsmanship.

The inspiration behind Arha Group began with a simple yet extraordinary thought: What if gold could become more than jewelry? What if it could be experienced as a part of everyday life—from pillow covers to apparel, woven into the fabric of luxury itself?

Driven by this vision, Swathi embarked on a journey to transform an idea into reality. But Arha Group became more than a luxury brand. It evolved into a mission to revive traditional weaving techniques, preserve India's rich cultural heritage, and create sustainable opportunities for skilled artisans whose craftsmanship deserved recognition and respect.

Today, Arha Group stands as a symbol of heritage, innovation, and timeless elegance—where every creation reflects a commitment to preserving tradition while redefining luxury for generations to come.
